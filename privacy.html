MakesSense Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 22, 2026

MakesSense is a teaching-first math learning application. We respect your privacy and design the app to minimize data collection.

Information We Collect

MakesSense does not require users to create an account. We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Information Stored on Your Device

MakesSense may store certain information locally on your device to support app features:

  • Solved problem history
  • App preferences (appearance settings)

This information is stored only on your device and is not transmitted externally.

Subscriptions and Payments

Subscriptions are handled through Apple’s App Store. We do not collect payment data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share personal information.

Children’s Privacy

MakesSense is designed for educational use. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy over time.
